**Date:** 2026-11-19, 14:28-22:29 UTC. All 54 runs reached their window's end with the source's block hash. The 18 replay runs logged 0 ERROR lines. Nothing was rerun.

**Figures:** each figure is the change in the median of one arm against the median of its baseline, in percent. A figure in **bold** means the two arms' ranges do not overlap. "No measurable change" means they do. Medians, ranges and the ratios behind every figure are in [score.txt](score.txt), which the commands below regenerate.

**Why the cap moves the replay window:** the cap applies to a whole verification call when its largest proof has 8 or more inputs. With it, the node averaged about 5.3 cores over the window, against 6.3 for stock, so it engaged on the 12 blocks with 128-input transactions. This also explains why the one-multiexp set gains less here than predicted: its baseline, CpTW, already carries the cap.
